# ASEAN QUANTUAM TALK

![ASEAN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/limlining/SECP-TEKNOLOGY-INFORMATION-SYSTEM/main/photo_2025-12-17_21-48-32.jpg)

REFLECTION:

A better idea of how daily lives could be affected by quantum technology and how cities like Johor could be helped to grow into tech hubs was gained by attending the ASEAN Quantum Summit at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia on 11 December 2025. It was learned that quantum computing, quantum communication, and quantum sensing are included in quantum technology.

Complex problems are solved much faster than by regular computers through the use of qubits and superposition in quantum computing. Information transfer is made super secure by quantum communication, and things can be measured very precisely by quantum sensing, which is found useful for things like GPS-free navigation and advanced medical imaging.

Inspiration was also drawn from how quantum research is planned carefully and is linked to real-world applications by countries in ASEAN, like Singapore. It was realized that advanced technology can be excelled in even by smaller nations if planning and teamwork are focused on. A motivation was felt to keep up with quantum technology and for thoughts to be given to how innovation could be contributed to in the future.
